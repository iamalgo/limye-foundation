Who We Serve

Limyè Foundation serves veterans, SSI recipients, seniors, at-risk youth, and families in need of housing and support. Our integrated approach bridges critical gaps in housing, access to care, and healthy food.

Veterans & Military Families

Building futures through education, mentorship, and life skills.

Support for those experiencing homelessness and housing insecurity.
