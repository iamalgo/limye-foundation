Shared Vision, Shared Impact

"Like a porch light that guides travelers home, we illuminate pathways to health, dignity, and opportunity."

🏠 New Heights Mission

Provides wrap-around services through partnerships with local nonprofits to support TCS students, families, and the broader Tuscaloosa community with education, homelessness prevention, youth services, mental health, and healthcare.

💡 Limye' Foundation Mission

Addresses critical, interconnected health and social needs through integrated services supporting individuals and families across the lifespan with housing assistance, transportation support, and nutrition programs.

Perfect Partnership Alignment

Both organizations believe in integrated, community-driven solutions that address root causes rather than symptoms, empowering families to thrive through coordinated support services.

Our Three Pillars of Community Support

Comprehensive services that directly complement New Heights' existing partner network

🏠

Stable Housing

Supportive housing solutions combining safe, affordable housing with wrap-around services including case management, healthcare access, mental health support, and life skills training.

Aligns with: New Heights' homelessness prevention focus
🚐

Transportation Access

Facilitated Non-Emergency Medical Transportation (NEMT) service providing "white-glove" assistance to remove barriers to healthcare, education, employment, and essential services.

Fills Gap: Transportation services not currently offered at New Heights
🍽️

Nutrition Security

Medically Tailored Meals (MTM) program providing home delivery of customized nutrition for individuals with chronic conditions, food insecurity, and special dietary needs.

Enhances: New Heights' food distribution through West Alabama Food Bank

Addressing Critical Community Needs

23% Diabetes rate in some West Alabama counties - among nation's highest
17% Of adults facing food insecurity
23% Of children lacking regular access to nutritious food
5,000+ Projected individuals served annually by Year 3

The Interconnected Crisis

Transportation barriers prevent access to healthcare and nutritious food. Food insecurity worsens chronic conditions. Housing instability makes disease management nearly impossible. Our integrated approach breaks these vicious cycles.

Theory of Change

Meeting Immediate Needs → Building Stability → Creating Opportunities → Achieving Transformation

Partnership Benefits for New Heights

🤝 Service Integration

Seamless referrals between New Heights partners and Limye' programs, creating a true "one-stop shop" for families

🚀 Expanded Capacity

Additional services (transportation, specialized nutrition) without New Heights bearing operational costs

📊 Shared Data & Outcomes

Collaborative tracking of family outcomes across multiple service areas for enhanced impact measurement

💰 Resource Optimization

Shared infrastructure costs, joint funding opportunities, and enhanced community visibility

Operational Synergies

Our coordinated care teams, warm handoff protocols, and integrated data systems eliminate service gaps and reduce client burden while maximizing both organizations' impact.

Partnership Implementation Plan

Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-6)

  • Secure dedicated office space at New Heights
  • Finalize 501(c)(3) nonprofit status
  • Establish referral protocols with existing partners
  • Launch pilot transportation services

Phase 2: Integration (Months 7-12)

  • Full NEMT service launch
  • Begin medically tailored meals program
  • Integrate data systems for coordinated care
  • Joint staff training and service coordination

Phase 3: Expansion (Year 2+)

  • Launch supportive housing initiatives
  • Expand services to surrounding counties
  • Joint fundraising and grant applications
  • Community impact measurement and reporting

Why Office Space at New Heights is Critical

Co-location with New Heights partners allows us to coordinate directly, cut barriers for families, and amplify community impact.
