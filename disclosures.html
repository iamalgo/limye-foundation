Legal & Regulatory Disclosures

Transparency, TCPA Compliance & Non-Profit Governance

1. Non-Profit Status

Limyè Foundation (also operating in coordination with Porch Light Foundation) is an Alabama nonprofit organization organized for charitable and educational purposes to empower vulnerable populations through supportive housing, transportation, and nutrition. 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status filing is actively pending.

2. TCPA / 10DLC Mobile Messaging Disclosure

By providing your mobile number through our online forms, you consent to receive informational and transactional SMS messages from Limyè Foundation regarding your inquiries, intake, appointment reminders, and supportive services.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You may revoke consent at any time by replying STOP to any message. For assistance, reply HELP or contact our intake desk at info@limyefoundation.org, call toll-free at 1-866-546-9311 (1-866-LIMYE-11), or direct helpline at (205) 300-9531.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. We never sell, lease, or share mobile subscriber data with third-party marketers.

3. Independent Living Housing Model

Limyè Independent Living Homes provide stable, community-oriented housing for veterans, seniors, and independent adults. Our residences are unlicensed living environments operating under Alabama landlord-tenant regulations and are not certified as assisted living, medical care, or nursing facilities.

4. Equal Housing Opportunity & Non-Discrimination

Limyè Foundation is committed to equal opportunity and complies with the Fair Housing Act and Civil Rights laws. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, or veteran status.
