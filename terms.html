2. Description of Services

Limyè Foundation is a charitable organization operating in West Alabama and North Alabama dedicated to providing supportive housing programs, healthcare non-emergency medical transportation (NEMT), nutritional support, and related community empowerment services to vulnerable populations, including veterans, seniors, SSI recipients, and low-income families.
