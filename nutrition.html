Food as Medicine

Plates for Patriots - Nourishing Health and Independence

Limyè's Nutrition Program delivers medically tailored meals to veterans, seniors, and individuals with chronic health conditions. Food is more than a meal—it's a path to better health and independence.

Coming Late 2025

Pilot meal deliveries starting with veterans in Tuscaloosa. Sign up for nutrition program alerts to be the first to know when we launch.

Program Highlights

Chef-Prepared Meals

Meals designed by registered dietitians and prepared by professional chefs

Condition-Specific

Medically tailored meals for diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease, and more

Direct Delivery

Home delivery through our NEMT network - no transportation barriers

Who We Serve

Veterans

  • VA-referred veterans with chronic conditions
  • Post-discharge nutrition support
  • Medication adherence support through proper nutrition
  • Integration with VA healthcare teams

Seniors & High-Risk Individuals

  • Seniors with limited mobility or cooking ability
  • Individuals with chronic health conditions
  • Post-hospital discharge nutrition support
  • Food insecurity prevention

Health Conditions We Address

Diabetes

Blood sugar management meals

Heart Disease

Low-sodium, heart-healthy options

Kidney Disease

Renal-friendly nutrition plans

Obesity/Malnutrition

Weight management support

PTSD/Mental Health

Mood-supporting nutrition

Mobility Issues

Easy-to-eat, nutrient-dense meals

Medication Support

Meals that enhance medication effectiveness

Cancer Care

Treatment-supportive nutrition

Expected Health Outcomes

Improved Health Metrics

Better blood sugar control, blood pressure management, and overall health indicators

Reduced Hospitalizations

Fewer emergency department visits and hospital readmissions through better nutrition

Enhanced Medication Adherence

Proper nutrition supporting medication effectiveness and reducing side effects
