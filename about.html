Our Mission – Empowerment Through Stability

Building pathways to independence for Alabama's most vulnerable populations

Mission Statement

Limyè Foundation empowers vulnerable populations, including our nation's veterans, through comprehensive services that bridge critical gaps in healthcare access, nutrition, housing, and community support, creating pathways to self-sufficiency and improved quality of life.

"Limyè" means "light" in Haitian Creole, symbolizing our commitment to lighting the way toward independence, dignity, and hope for those we serve.

Our Core Values

Every person deserves respect, compassion, and the opportunity to live with dignity

Empowering individuals to achieve independence and sustainable growth

Community Integration

Building connections and fostering belonging within the broader community

Populations We Serve

Veterans & Military Families

Comprehensive support for our nation's heroes, including housing assistance, healthcare transportation, and nutrition services tailored to veteran needs.

Seniors & SSI Recipients

Independent living support for elderly community members, ensuring dignity and accessibility while maintaining autonomy.

At-Risk Youth

Education, mentorship, and life skills training to build bright futures for young people facing challenges.

Individuals Experiencing Homelessness

Rapid rehousing, transitional support, and wraparound services to break the cycle of homelessness.

Clients with Chronic Health Conditions

Medically tailored nutrition and healthcare navigation for individuals managing ongoing health challenges.

Individuals in Recovery

Sober living environments and support services for those on their journey to sustained recovery and independence.
