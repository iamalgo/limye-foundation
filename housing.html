Independent Living Homes

Stability, Dignity, Community

Limyè Independent Living Homes are designed for veterans, seniors, and individuals in recovery who need affordable, stable housing. Our homes provide independence within a supportive community, without the high costs of assisted living or the restrictions of traditional shelters.

We follow a tenant-focused model, where residents sign leases, share costs fairly, and live by a clear set of community rules that ensure respect and safety for all.

Tenant Intake Form

Note: Limyè Independent Living Homes are unlicensed residences under Alabama landlord-tenant law. Residents remain independent, with access to community resources, case management, and transportation as needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is rent?

Rent is split fairly among tenants and may be offset by vouchers or assistance (HUD-VASH, SSI compliance).

What services are included?

Residents maintain independence but may receive reminders, access to social activities, and support with community transportation.

Are there rules?

Yes. We enforce clear house rules (no violence, no overnight guests, shared chores, quiet hours) to maintain harmony and safety.

Why Huntsville first?

Huntsville's zoning and housing market make it easier to launch independent living communities quickly while keeping rents affordable.

Will Tuscaloosa still happen?

Yes, Tuscaloosa homes are secured for Fall 2025. Huntsville is simply our faster launch market.
